What the Meditation Let In

She meditated with the stone under her tongue and went too far down. Four things she could try. Three of them are not going to work.

SAN 100
Rue

Focus… breathe… in… out… Something is standing between the bed and the window.

Mind-reading 50 · hard 25

What the roll decides. Mind-reading is the only action that resolves the scene, and it can be retried as often as the player likes — each attempt costs SAN on a failure, so the question is how much of herself she is willing to spend finding out what she is looking at. A regular success tells her what the shape is. A Hard success gives some SAN back, lights the window, and shows her one thing in the room that is not hers.
